A DIMO-powered Mileage Calculator web app would automate and simplify monthly mileage reporting for reimbursements, leveraging always-on R1 data and offering tangible user rewards—outperforming manual workflows and popular apps like MileIQ and Stride.[1][2][3]

### Key Features
- **Automatic, Background Mileage Capture:** Utilizes vehicle-connected R1 data for precision—no need to tap start/stop or rely on phone sensors. Trips are auto-logged whenever the vehicle is in motion, no manual effort required.[4]
- **Daily/Monthly Breakdown & CSV Export:** Users can generate a clean, IRS-compliant CSV for any date range, showing miles driven per day and optionally tagging drives as business, personal, or custom purposes.[5][1]
- **Simple Trip Review & Bulk Classification:** In-browser, users can quickly review, edit, and classify trips in bulk (e.g., all weekday trips as “Business”). Add notes or purposes as needed for extra context.[6]
- **Automated Reminders & Calendar Sync:** Sends monthly or weekly prompts to review and export logs. Syncs mileage periods with Google Calendar or popular work scheduling services like WhenIWork.[7]
- **Direct Integration & Rewards:** BD targets: easy API or file sync for QuickBooks (reimbursements), payroll, or gig-economy portals. Driving for work also earns additional DIMO rewards—offering higher value than passive logging alone.[3]
- **Privacy & Security:** Users can remove or redact personal trips before export and control exactly what data is shared or integrated with employer systems.[8][1]

### Pitch to Consumers and Partners
- **"You work. We track. You earn—automatically.”** More accurate than manual logs, more seamless than MileIQ or Stride, and with DIMO rewards on top.
- **Ideal for:** W-2 employees, freelancers, gig workers, rideshare/delivery drivers, and anyone who misses out on reimbursements due to reporting hassle.
- **Partnership/Channel Play:** Offer direct CSV/API connections to Quickbooks, WhenIWork, HR/payroll systems, or partner with channel apps like MileIQ and Stride to power their backend with DIMO’s device-verified data.[2][1]

This project maximizes user and business value with minimal friction, perfectly fits rapid hackathon execution, and showcases DIMO’s power to automate a high-pain, high-frequency user task.[1][2][3]
